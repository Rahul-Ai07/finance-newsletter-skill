SHEET 4: DEBT SERVICE
================================================================================

Purpose: Loan amortization schedule showing year-wise debt repayment

KEY COLUMNS:

Col A: Year (Year 1 to Year 7)

Col B: Opening Balance (Loan amount initially)
- Year 1: Rs. 7,14,000
- Year 2: Closing Balance from Year 1
- Pattern: Decreases each year as principal paid down
- Year 7: Should approach zero (Rs. ~5-10K remaining)

Col C: Annual EMI (Fixed)
- All years: Rs. 1,54,128 (= 12 × 12,844)
- This remains constant throughout tenure
- Formula: =Monthly_EMI × 12

Col D: Principal Portion (Increases each year)
- Year 1: Rs. 86,303 (Lower - more interest paid)
- Year 7: Rs. 145,904 (Higher - more principal paid)
- As interest decreases, principal increases
- Formula: =Annual_EMI - Interest_Portion

Col E: Interest Portion (Decreases each year)
- Year 1: Rs. 67,825 (Higher at start)
- Year 7: Rs. 8,124 (Lower at end)
- Calculated on opening balance
- Formula: =Opening_Balance × 0.095 (9.5% annual rate)

Col F: Closing Balance (Decreases each year)
- Year 1: Rs. 7,14,000 - Rs. 86,303 = Rs. 6,27,697
- Year 2: Rs. 6,27,697 - Rs. 97,170 = Rs. 5,30,527
- [Pattern continues until payoff]
- Year 7: Should be zero or very small amount
- Formula: =Opening_Balance - Principal_Paid

Col G: Cumulative Principal Paid (Increases)
- Tracks total principal repaid so far
- Year 1: Rs. 86,303
- Year 7: Rs. 7,14,000 (=Total Loan)
- Formula: =Previous_Cumulative + Current_Principal

Col H: Principal % of EMI
- Percentage of EMI going toward principal
- Year 1: ~56% (Principal / EMI)
- Year 7: ~95% (Principal / EMI)
- Shows shifting composition over time

Col I: Interest % of EMI
- Percentage of EMI going toward interest
- Year 1: ~44%
- Year 7: ~5%
- Decreases as loan is repaid

BOTTOM SUMMARY ROWS:

Row after amortization:
- TOTAL REPAYMENT: Rs. 10,78,896 (Sum of all annual EMIs)
- TOTAL INTEREST PAID: Rs. 3,64,896 (Rs. 10,78,896 - Rs. 7,14,000)

KEY INSIGHTS:

1. Interest Front-Loading
   - Early years: Majority of EMI is interest
   - Later years: Majority of EMI is principal
   - This is normal for reducing balance loans

2. Interest vs. Principal Split
   Year 1: 44% interest, 56% principal
   Year 7: 5% interest, 95% principal

   Borrower pays ~51% of principal as interest over 7 years

3. Total Interest Cost
   Rs. 3,64,896 represents the cost of borrowing
   - This is 51% of original loan amount
   - At 9.50% over 7 years, this is normal

HOW TO USE FOR CASH FLOW:

   Use Col E (Interest Portion) for tax deduction calculation

2. Cash Flow Projection:
   Use Col D (Principal) to calculate free cash flow available after debt service

3. Refinancing Decision:

SHEET 5: RATIOS & COVENANTS
================================================================================

Purpose: Key financial ratios and debt service covenant analysis

SECTION 1: KEY PROFITABILITY RATIOS

Row 3: Gross Profit Margin (%)
Formula: ((Revenue - COGS) / Revenue) × 100
Current: Links to Financials sheet
- Shows profitability after direct costs
- Expected range for transport: 18-22%

Row 5: EBITDA Margin (%)
Formula: (EBITDA / Revenue) × 100
Current: = (D6 / D3) × 100
- Primary metric for operations
- Shows operational profit generation
- Expected range for transport: 18-25%
- Current assumption: 30%

Row 7: Net Profit Margin (%)
Formula: (PAT / Revenue) × 100
Current: = (D11 / D3) × 100
- Shows bottom-line profitability
- After all expenses including interest & tax
- Expected range: 8-12%

SECTION 2: DEBT SERVICE RATIOS [CRITICAL]

Row 11: Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) ✓✓✓ MOST IMPORTANT
Formula: Annual EBITDA / Annual Debt Service Obligation
Current: = D6 / (Annual EMI)
Example: Rs. 168.75L / Rs. 1.54L = 109.5x

Interpretation:
- > 2.50x: Excellent credit (Low risk)
- 1.50-2.50x: Good (Acceptable)
- 1.00-1.50x: Marginal (Watch carefully)
- < 1.00x: REJECT (Cannot service debt)

Bank Requirement: Minimum 1.50x

Current Status: EXCEPTIONAL (>10x is extraordinary)
- This means borrower generates 10x the cash needed to pay EMI
- Substantial buffer for business downturns
- Very safe credit

Row 13: Interest Coverage Ratio (ICR)
Formula: EBIT / Annual Interest Expense
Current: = D6 / (Annual Interest)
- Shows how many times profit covers interest
- Higher is better
- Minimum acceptable: 2.50x
- Current status: Exceptional

Row 15: Loan to Value Ratio (LTV)
Formula: Loan Amount / Annual Revenue
Current: = 7.14 / (D3/100)
- Shows loan size relative to business scale
- Typical acceptable: < 50%
- Current status: ~1.4% LTV (Excellent)

SECTION 3: REPAYMENT CAPACITY ANALYSIS

Row 19: Annual EMI
- Total cash needed each year for loan repayment
- Fixed at Rs. 1,54,128 for this loan

Row 21: Annual EBITDA (Lakh)
- Cash available from operations
- Links to Financials sheet

Row 23: Available for Debt Service (Lakh)
- Theoretical cash after capex and working capital
- Shows cushion for debt payment

Row 25: Debt Service Capacity
- Multiple calculated as EBITDA / Annual EMI
- Shows how many times can service debt
- Higher is safer

MONITORING THESE RATIOS:

During Loan Tenure:

Monthly: Check if EMI paid on time (DSCR not needed monthly)

Quarterly:
- Recalculate DSCR with latest financial data
- If DSCR < 2.00x: Flag for review
- If DSCR < 1.50x: Activate covenant breach protocol

Half-yearly:
- Full ratio analysis
- Trend analysis (improving or declining)
- Compare with industry benchmarks
- Trigger early warning if concerning

Annually:
- Complete rerating
- Update projections
- Covenant assessment
- Refinancing opportunities evaluation

COVENANT BREACHES:

If DSCR drops below 1.50x:
1. Immediate contact with borrower
2. Request explanation and action plan
3. Audit of financial statements
4. Site visit to assess business condition
5. Consider debt restructuring options
6. May trigger demand for additional security

Red Flag Scenarios (Trigger Special Review):
- 2 consecutive quarters with DSCR < 1.75x
- Single quarter DSCR < 1.50x
- DSCR trending downward consistently
- ICR drops below 2.00x
- Margin compression > 5%

IMPORTANT NOTES ON FORMULAS
================================================================================

1. ALL FORMULAS USE RELATIVE REFERENCES
   - When you change base revenue, all projections update
   - When you change growth rate, all years recalculate
   - This is by design for ease of sensitivity analysis

2. DO NOT DELETE COLUMNS
   - Each column represents a year
   - Deletion breaks formulas in other sheets
   - If you want fewer years, hide columns instead

3. CIRCULAR REFERENCES AVOIDED
   - Interest in Financials sheet not linked to Debt Service
   - This would create circular calculation
   - Keep as static values or manually update

4. COPY FORMULAS CAREFULLY
   - If extending projections beyond 7 years, copy formulas
   - Ensure column letters auto-increment
   - Test at least 2 rows to verify

5. BACKUP ORIGINAL
   - Keep original copy unchanged
   - Save analysis version separately
   - Allows comparison between scenarios

================================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING COMMON ISSUES
================================================================================

Issue 1: "Circular Reference" error
Cause: Usually from linking Interest to Debt Service sheet
Fix: Keep Interest as manual entry in Financials sheet

Issue 2: Formulas showing as text (#VALUE! error)
Cause: Cell formatted as text instead of number
Fix: Right-click → Format Cells → Choose Number format

Issue 3: Growth not applying to all years
Cause: Formula not copied to all columns
Fix: Select formula cell, copy, paste to remaining years

Issue 4: DSCR calculation incorrect
Cause: Wrong row reference in formula
Fix: Verify formula references correct EBITDA and EMI rows

Issue 5: Column widths too narrow for data
Cause: Auto-fit not applied
Fix: Double-click column border to auto-fit

Issue 6: Charts not updating
Cause: Chart not linked to dynamic range
Fix: Delete and recreate chart with proper range
