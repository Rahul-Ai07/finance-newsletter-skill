## Published criteria

- **State Bank of India, Specialist Cadre Officers, Digital Marketing Department (Deputy Vice President and other posts):** Registration extended to 31 Oct 2026 (original window 30 Sep to 21 Oct 2026). Posts include Deputy Vice President (Digital Marketing), Deputy Vice President (Analytical Marketing and Campaign, CTC up to Rs 63 lakh a year) and Content Specialist. Contract of up to 5 years (3 years, renewable for 2). Selection by shortlisting, interview and CTC negotiation.
- **Department of Financial Services (Ministry of Finance), Presiding Officer, Debts Recovery Tribunals:** DFS vacancies page: selection notice published 1 Oct 2026, due 2 Nov 2026; a corrigendum published 10 Oct 2026 is listed with due date 9 Nov 2026. The 2026 eligibility text is not in the text read. The 2025 notice for the same post listed: a sitting or former High Court judge; an Indian Legal Service member with 10 years' litigation experience who has held an Additional Secretary-level post; or a person who has held the office of DRT Presiding Officer for 3 years.
- **State Bank of India, Deputy General Manager (AI Governance and Compliance), fixed term:** Apply online 19 Oct to 9 Nov 2026; cut-off date 30 Sep 2026. Education: B.E./B.Tech/M.Tech in Computer Science, IT, Electronics, Electrical and Electronics, or Electronics and Communication, or equivalent; or M.Sc (Statistics or Computer Science), MA (Statistics), M Stat or MCA. Preferred: MBA/PGDM with finance specialisation; certification in ML/AI, NLP, web crawling or neural networks. Experience: 10+ years in banking, BFSI or IT, including 2+ years in a senior leadership role in AI governance, responsible AI, model risk management, regulatory compliance, data privacy, information security or technology risk and business transformation. Individual-contributor experience does not count toward senior leadership. Age: 35 to 50 (as read from the advertisement table).
- **State Bank of India, Deputy General Manager (Risk and Operational Analytics), fixed term:** Apply online 19 Oct to 9 Nov 2026; cut-off date 30 Sep 2026. Age 35 to 50. Experience: 10+ years in banking, BFSI or IT, including 2+ years in a senior leadership role covering risk analytics, operational analytics, AI/ML model oversight and model governance.
- **State Bank of India, General Manager and Chief AI Officer, fixed term:** Apply online 19 Oct to 9 Nov 2026; cut-off date 30 Sep 2026.
- **State Bank of India, Deputy General Manager (Data Engineering, Model Application Development and AI Ops), fixed term:** Apply online 19 Oct to 9 Nov 2026; cut-off date 30 Sep 2026.

- **State Bank of India, Enterprise AI Architect:** Apply online 19 Oct to 9 Nov 2026; cut-off date 30 Sep 2026.
- **IIFCL, Deputy General Manager (Grade D), regular:** Advertisement dated 3 Sep 2026. Essential: CA, CMA (ICMAI) or MBA (Finance or equivalent). Experience: minimum 15 years in officer or executive cadre, of which at least 10 years in project finance of large corporates, treasury operations, risk management, HR operations and training, or finance and accounts in banks, financial institutions, PSUs or corporates. No application fee.

Qualification: CA, MBA (Finance), ICWA or CFA, with a minimum of 15 years' experience. Upper age limit 60 on the publication date. Officers of central or state government bodies and PSUs may be considered on deputation; power-sector CPSU candidates with IPO experience get priority.
