## 1. Mission and operating contract

Prioritize **deputation**, followed by senior lateral/regular, fixed-term contractual, advisory/consulting, secondment and board-level appointments. Identify both **AGM and DGM** posts and their defensible functional equivalents, and the equivalent senior grades in PSUs, tribunals and international bodies. A role must not be deemed suitable merely because its title sounds senior.

Prevent recurrence by searching **both advertisements and individual role titles**, including AGM, DGM, GM, Chief, Head, Lead, Specialist, Advisor, Programme Manager, Risk Officer and AI Governance. Inspect every active recruitment PDF for *all posts in the advertisement*, including separate advertisements and addenda. Do not assert that a post exists, is open, or is eligible without checking its official notice.

It is not IBBI. **IBBI** (Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India) is covered separately under S2. IDBI Bank and DICGC are also covered under S1.

3. **Banking and finance domains:** retail banking, agriculture, rural finance, priority sector, MSME, credit, portfolio risk, NPA, stressed assets, SARFAESI, DRT, recovery, AML, KYC, compliance, financial inclusion, fintech, innovation, startup ecosystem, financial sector development.
4. **Employment types:** recruitment, vacancy, notification, circular, deputation, on deputation, absorption, contract, lateral, lateral entry, specialist officer, regular, advisory, roster, consultancy, expression of interest, corrigendum, extension of date.

**Coverage check:** For each segment report `checked_official_site`, `checked_advertisement_PDF`, `checked_corrigenda`, `checked_application_portal`, `last_checked_IST`, `limitations`. Mark `NOT VERIFIED` rather than `NO OPENINGS` if coverage is incomplete.

## 5. Source verification and evidence hierarchy

1. **Primary:** official institution careers page, original PDF, corrigendum, application portal and official HR contact.
2. **Secondary:** verified employer-hosted job listing or authoritative government vacancy index.
3. **Discovery only:** job aggregators, newspapers, social media and reposts; follow through to official source before calling a vacancy confirmed. Application portals run by third parties (for example FactoHR) are not official sources. Confirm the posting on the employer's own site, and check the domain. The RBI has warned of fake appointment letters sent from look-alike domains.

Capture: exact title; advert/reference number; institution; segment; slot; posting location; mode; pay/grade if published; opening date; deadline **with timezone**; cutoff date; mandatory education (including accepted branches); essential experience and leadership years; desirable credentials; age limits/relaxations; deputation cadre-equivalence rules; application route; documents; fee; official deep link; PDF link; last verified timestamp; status.

A vacancy is `VERIFIED OPEN` only when the official application window is current **and** the posting has not been withdrawn/filled. Other statuses: `LISTED` (on the institution's current official listing, deadline not read), `UPCOMING`, `CLOSING SOON`, `CLOSED`, `SELECTION IN PROGRESS`, `WITHDRAWN`, `UNVERIFIED`. Do not mistake a list of vacancies anticipated up to a date for an application deadline; read the application-submission clause separately.

**Source confidence (v3.1):** every record carries `source_confidence`: `OFFICIAL_DIRECT` (page or PDF opened), `OFFICIAL_INDEXED` (official text returned by a search restricted to the institution's own domain, with the official URL), `AGGREGATOR` or `USER_PROVIDED`. Only the two official levels may set `verified: true`. When a direct fetch fails, run WebSearch with `allowed_domains` set to the official domain before falling back to aggregators. Indian banks now use `.bank.in` domains (for example sbi.bank.in, idbi.bank.in); search both old and new domains.

**Blocked sources (v3 rule):** If an official host refuses the connection (for example, a 403 from the environment network policy), record `blocked_403` for that source, mark every affected record `UNVERIFIED`, and mark the segment `NOT VERIFIED`. Never report a blocked segment as "no openings". Name the blocked host in the digest so the user can allow it in environment settings.

## 6. Eligibility gate — apply in order

**Gate A: hard disqualifiers.** Degree/discipline, age cutoff, required years of experience, mandatory hands-on specialization, sector/employer restrictions, nationality, grade/cadre equivalence, work authorization, application route, deadline. Quote the *shortest necessary* exact criterion with official citation.

**Gate B: equivalence and ambiguity.** MBA Business Analytics does not automatically substitute for a technical or finance-specific postgraduate degree. B.Tech Agricultural Engineering is an engineering degree but does not automatically meet a restricted branch list. Outskill certificates evidence training, **not** professional AI implementation. Scale IV for eight years does not automatically confer DGM eligibility or PSB pay equivalence. Record ambiguities and recommend official HR clarification; do not present them as confirmed eligibility.

**Gate C: experience fit.** Distinguish banking operations, SARFAESI/DRT legal recovery, governance, analytics study, AI project ownership, AI deployment, production model validation, regulatory oversight, budget and people leadership. Do not combine adjacent experiences to fabricate a mandatory number of years.

**Gate D: opportunity value.** Evaluate seniority, strategic trajectory, functional fit, location, contract security, compensation, deputation permission and career-drift risk.

## 7. Scoring rubric (100 points, only after eligibility gates)

- Banking-domain relevance: 20
- Seniority/leadership equivalence: 20
- Essential qualification/experience evidence: 20
- AI/analytics/governance alignment: 15
- Employment preference (deputation first): 10
- Location preference: 10
- Deadline/application feasibility: 5

**Interpretation:** 85–100 `STRONG MATCH`; 70–84 `POTENTIAL MATCH`; 50–69 `STRETCH`; below 50 `LOW PRIORITY`. Any unresolved mandatory criterion overrides numerical score to `ELIGIBILITY UNCONFIRMED`; known failure to satisfy a mandatory condition = `NOT ELIGIBLE`, regardless of score. If job represents material downgrade or a move away from career objectives, mark `CAREER-DRIFT RISK`. Records that are `UNVERIFIED` are not scored; they stay `NOT SCORED` until the official notice is read.

**AI role calibration:** AI Governance/Responsible AI/AI Risk may be a stronger functional fit than ML engineering or MLOps, **but only if the actual advertisement accepts the candidate's education and requires experience they can evidence**. Do not equate foundational AI coursework with enterprise AI leadership.

## 9. Delta detection, escalation and missed-vacancy audit

Compare each run with the preceding verified register. Emit `NEW`, `UPDATED`, `CLOSING SOON`, `REOPENED`, `CLOSED`, `WITHDRAWN`, `NO CHANGE` and `NEEDS VERIFICATION`. Highlight deadlines in 7 days and **urgent** deadlines in 72 hours (thresholds in `config/schedule.json`). If a strong match is discovered after publication, record `MISSED EARLIER`, why (e.g., PDF not parsed, AGM/DGM synonym missed, separate advert omitted), and a concrete correction to search coverage. Never falsely claim an earlier scan was comprehensive.

When user identifies an advertisement not previously captured, **audit immediately**: find official source, inspect every role, add separate entries, reassess eligibility, and update keyword coverage. State uncertainty if the document cannot be retrieved.

## 10. Standard output format

**Header:** `Senior Banking Opportunity Watch | as of DD MMM YYYY, HH:MM IST` and sources checked, including blocked sources.

**Segments:** list each segment from 3A (S1 to S8), grouped by slot where useful.

For each actionable vacancy: `Institution — Exact title | Segment | Location | Mode | Status | Deadline | Eligibility: Confirmed/Possible/Unconfirmed/No | Match tier | Why it fits | Main gap | Official application link`.

Then: `Priority actions` (maximum 3); `Unverified leads` (brief); `Coverage limitations` (brief, including blocked hosts); `Changes since previous scan` (NEW/UPDATED/CLOSING SOON etc.). Avoid generic listings or unverified claims of no openings. If none are verified, say **"No currently open strong match verified in the sources checked"**, not **"There are no vacancies."**

The engine renders this format to `opportunity-radar/reports/YYYY-MM-DD.md`.

## 11. SBI AI advertisement deep-dive checklist

- Locate official current-openings entry and exact advert number; record date and correction notices.
- Download and read **full** detailed notification; enumerate all GM/DGM/AGM/other AI posts separately.
- Compare each post's age cutoff, academic discipline list, post-qualification experience, years in AI, governance, analytics, data science and senior management.
- Assess B.Tech **Agricultural Engineering** literally against permitted engineering branches and any "equivalent qualification" wording.
- Assess MBA **Business Analytics** as its own credential; check whether accepted as an alternative or only desirable.
- Record Outskill AI Catalyst/Generalist training as supplemental certificates; do not use them to satisfy mandated production AI experience.
- Determine if NPA/SARFAESI/DRT/compliance experience directly maps to AI risk, governance, financial crime or credit-risk model oversight; ask for project-level evidence if needed.
- Compare AGM and DGM routes side-by-side, including potential grade step-down, fixed-term contract conditions and compensation.
- If qualification ambiguity persists, draft a short HR clarification request with the exact clause and qualifications before fee payment.

## 13. Tool and automation behavior

For a **current vacancy check**, browse official sources live and cite them; never substitute a past answer for current verification. If tools fail, explicitly disclose the limitation. For **scheduled monitoring**, use an actual automation with the user's requested cadence; a SKILL.md file alone does not execute itself.

**Scheduled alternate-day scan (v3):**

- State persists by commit, not by session memory. Each scan commits its run file, the register and the digest to branch `ccr-5b8902fd-3ed8ur`.

## 16. Engine integration (v3)

One scan run follows these steps, in this order:

1. `python3 opportunity-radar/engine/cadence.py` — exit 1 means stop.
2. Read `config/segments.json`. For each segment, run its `seed_queries` plus the keyword families in section 4. Attempt each official source with WebFetch. Record the result of every attempt.
3. Write `opportunity-radar/state/runs/YYYY-MM-DD.json` in the format described in `opportunity-radar/README.md`. Set `verified: true` only for records read from an official source in this run.
4. `python3 opportunity-radar/engine/register.py opportunity-radar/state/runs/YYYY-MM-DD.json` — updates the register, applies the status rules and writes the digest.
5. `python3 -m unittest discover -s opportunity-radar/tests` — must pass.
6. Commit the run file, register and digest to `ccr-5b8902fd-3ed8ur` and push.

The engine enforces the rules that are mechanical: stable IDs, no `VERIFIED OPEN` without an official source, past deadlines become `CLOSED`, and missing records become `NEEDS VERIFICATION`. Judgement stays with the scan: eligibility verdicts, match tiers and priority actions.
