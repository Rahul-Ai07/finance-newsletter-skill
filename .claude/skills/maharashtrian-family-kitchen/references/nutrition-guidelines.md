# Nutrition Guidelines for the Family

Based on the principles of the ICMR–NIN *Dietary Guidelines for Indians* ("My Plate for the Day") adapted to Maharashtrian home food. These are general healthy-eating targets, not medical prescriptions.

## 1. The balanced plate, Maharashtrian style

| Plate share | Food group | Maharashtrian foods |
|---|---|---|
| ~50% | Vegetables, greens, fruit | bhaji (sukhi/patal), palebhaji, koshimbir, kachumber, seasonal fruit |
| ~25% | Grains — at least half whole grain / millet | jowar, bajra, nachni bhakri; whole-wheat poli; hand-pounded/ambemohar/indrayani rice in moderation |
| ~20% | Protein | varan, amti, usal, pithla, zunka, matki, chawli, kulith, paneer, egg, fish, chicken |
| ~5% + side | Dairy, nuts, seeds, fat | taak, dahi, milk; shengdana, til, javas, khobra chutney; a little tup (ghee) |

Simple rule for every meal: **"Bhaji double, bhakri-poli single, varan/usal never missing."**

## 2. Approximate daily portions (household measures)

1 katori = ~150 ml bowl. 1 poli = ~30 g atta. 1 bhakri = ~40–45 g flour.

| Food | Adult (moderate activity) | Child 4–6 yrs | Child 7–12 yrs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grains (poli/bhakri/bhaat/poha etc.) | 5–7 poli-equivalents | 2–3 | 3–5 |
| Pulses / dal / usal (cooked) | 2–3 katori | 1 katori | 1.5–2 katori |
| Vegetables (cooked + salad) | 3–4 katori | 1.5 katori | 2–3 katori |
| Green leafy veg | 1 katori (most days) | ½ katori | ½–1 katori |
| Fruit | 1–2 medium | 1 | 1–2 |
| Milk / curd / taak | 2 cups (~300 ml) | 2–3 cups (~400–500 ml) | 2–3 cups |
| Nuts & seeds | 1 small handful (~20–30 g) | 1 tbsp powder | 1–2 tbsp |
| Egg / fish / chicken (if non-veg) | 1 egg or 60–80 g fish/chicken on non-veg days | ½–1 egg / 30–40 g | 1 egg / 50 g |

Protein guide: adults roughly 0.8–1 g per kg body weight per day; school-age children need proportionally more for growth — that's why protein at breakfast matters.

## 3. Daily limits for the family

| Item | Adult per day | Child per day | Practical tip |
|---|---|---|---|
| Salt | < 5 g (1 level tsp, **including** pickles, papad, chutney) | 2–3 g | Salt at the end of cooking; keep loncha (pickle) and papad for weekends |
| Added sugar / jaggery | < 25 g (5 tsp) | < 15–20 g | Use gul (jaggery) in small amounts; skip sugar in kids' milk, add a date or banana |
| Visible oil + ghee | ~4–5 tsp (20–25 ml) | ~3 tsp | Measure phodni with a teaspoon, not a pour; 1 tsp tup on rice/poli is fine |
| Fried snacks | max 1×/week | max 1×/week | Air-fry / appe-pan versions of vada and bhaji |
| Maida / bakery (pav, biscuits) | max 1–2×/week | max 1–2×/week | Whole-wheat pav when possible |

## 4. The daily balance checklist (run on every plan)

- [ ] Protein source at breakfast, lunch and dinner
- [ ] Millet (jowar/bajra/nachni) at least once
- [ ] Green leafy vegetable (5 days/week target)
- [ ] At least 2 different coloured vegetables
- [ ] Raw component: koshimbir / kachumber / salad / sprouts
- [ ] 1–2 fruits per person
- [ ] Dairy: taak/dahi/milk — kids 2–3 servings
- [ ] Healthy fat: nuts, seeds, chutney (shengdana / til / javas)
- [ ] Fermented / probiotic: dahi, taak, idli/dhokla batter, amboli
- [ ] Fried ≤ weekly quota; sugar and salt within limits
- [ ] Water: adults 8–10 glasses, kids 6–8 (more in summer — kokum sarbat, taak, limbu pani without much sugar)

## 5. Key nutrients & where they come from (Maharashtrian sources)

| Nutrient | Why it matters | Best everyday sources |
|---|---|---|
| Protein | Growth (kids), muscles, fullness | dal, usal, matki/moong sprouts, paneer, dahi, egg, fish, peanuts, besan |
| Iron | Energy, focus; many Indian women & kids are low | nachni, bajra, methi, palak, ambadi, chawli leaves, kulith, til, gul, poha (with lemon) |
| Vitamin C (helps iron absorption) | Immunity, iron uptake | limbu on poha/usal, awla, peru (guava), santra, tomato, kokum |
| Calcium | Bones & teeth (kids!) | milk, dahi, paneer, nachni (very high), til, rajgira, leafy greens |
| Fibre | Digestion, blood sugar | bhakri, whole pulses, vegetables, fruit, javas |
| Omega-3 | Brain, heart | javas (flaxseed) chutney, walnuts, fish (bangda/mackerel, sardine/tarli) |
| Vitamin B12 | Nerves, energy (vegetarians are often low) | milk, dahi, paneer, eggs, fish — vegetarians: dairy daily, discuss supplements with doctor |
| Vitamin D | Bones | morning sunlight 15–20 min, eggs, fish, fortified milk |
| Zinc | Immunity, growth | pulses, til, pumpkin seeds, whole grains |

**Iron trick:** have vitamin C (limbu, awla, peru) with iron-rich meals; keep tea/coffee at least 1 hour away from meals.

## 6. Kids — special notes

- Breakfast before school is non-negotiable; include protein + complex carb (e.g., moong dhirde, thalipeeth with dahi, egg-poli roll, nachni satva).
- Tiffin: dry, non-soggy, no curd for long hours; finger food works best (thalipeeth wedges, paratha rolls, idli, dhokla, poha cutlet).
- Picky eaters: hide greens in thalipeeth, paratha, dhirde and pithla; let kids help shape and garnish; offer a new vegetable alongside a favourite.
- Limit packaged juice, chips, biscuits and chocolates; keep a "healthy snack jar": roasted chana, makhana, shengdana chikki (small piece), dink/methi-free dry fruit laddoo, rajgira chikki.
- Spice: take kids' portion out before adding extra chilli; balance with dahi or tup.

## 7. Adults — special notes

- Portion control on rice and poli at dinner; prefer bhakri, khichdi, soups, usal at night.
- 30+ years: emphasise calcium, fibre and protein; minimise fried snacks with tea.
- If anyone has diabetes, high BP, cholesterol, thyroid or kidney issues: apply general healthy-plate rules (millets, more vegetables, less salt/oil/sugar) and **advise consulting their doctor or a registered dietitian** for specific targets. Do not prescribe.

## 8. Healthier swaps (without losing taste)

| Instead of | Try |
|---|---|
| Maida pav every day | Whole-wheat pav, bhakri, or poli |
| Sabudana vada (fried) | Sabudana-peanut thalipeeth on tawa, or appe-pan vada |
| Batata vada | Appe-pan batata vada / baked vada; add peas & carrot to the filling |
| Sugar in tea / milk | Less sugar gradually; jaggery or date in kids' milk |
| Plain white rice | Mix with brown/hand-pounded rice, or vegetable masale bhaat with peas |
| Packaged namkeen | Roasted poha chivda, roasted makhana, chana-shengdana mix |
| Cream in gravies | Cashew-khobra paste or blended curd |
| Daily papad & loncha | Kachumber, fresh limbu, or a fresh chutney |
