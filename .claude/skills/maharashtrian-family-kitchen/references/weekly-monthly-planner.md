## 3. Sunday 1-hour prep plan (saves 20–30 min every weekday)

1. Soak & sprout: 1 cup matki + 1 cup moong (ready Tue/Wed).
2. Wash, dry & store greens in cotton cloth/paper inside a box (lasts 4–5 days).
3. Chop & store: onions (2 days only), carrots, beans, cabbage in boxes.
4. Make: ginger-garlic paste (1 week), green chutney (3 days, freeze cubes), 1 jar chutney powder.
5. Boil 4–5 potatoes (2 days).
6. Knead poli dough for Monday (fridge, 1 day max).
7. Plan the week's tiffins on a sticky note on the fridge.

**Night-before habit (5 min):** soak dal/rajma/sabudana as needed, defrost fish/chicken in fridge, keep nashta ingredients ready.

## 4. Monthly plan

### Monthly overview
| Week | Menu | Extra focus |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | A | Restock pantry (bulk list below), make masalas |
| Week 2 | B | Try one new recipe the kids choose |
| Week 3 | C | Pickle/chutney powder refresh |
| Week 4 | D | Review: what did the family love/skip? Update `family-profile.md` |

Then mark festivals and fasting days for the month (see `guests-festivals-seasons.md`) and swap those days' menus.

### Monthly kitchen tasks
- **Goda masala** (makes ~250 g, lasts 3 months): dry-roast separately on low heat — ½ cup coriander seeds, 2 tbsp jeera, 1 tbsp til, ¼ cup dry khobra (grated), 10 dried red chillies (bedgi), 8 cloves, 2-inch dalchini, 6 tej patta, 1 tsp shahjeera, 1 tsp mirey (pepper), 4 badiyan (star anise), 2 dagadphool (stone flower), 1 tsp hing, 6 velchi (cardamom), 1 tsp nagkesar (optional). Cool, grind fine. Store airtight.
- **Kanda-lasun masala** (Kolhapuri style, make in summer): onion & garlic slow-fried, khobra, til, whole spices, Lavangi/Bedgi chilli powder, salt — make in bulk or buy a trusted brand.
- **Thalipeeth bhajani** (lasts 2 months): roast separately — 2 cups jowar, 1 cup bajra, 1 cup rice, ½ cup wheat, ½ cup chana dal, ¼ cup urad dal, 2 tbsp jeera, 2 tbsp dhane → grind at chakki.
- **Metkut** (for varan-bhaat & kids): roasted chana dal, urad dal, rice, wheat, jeera, dhane, halad, hing → fine powder.
- **Chutney powders:** shengdana-lasun, javas, til, karala — one fresh jar every 2 weeks.
- **Flours:** get jowar/bajra/nachni ground fresh every 2–3 weeks (millet flours go rancid faster than wheat).
