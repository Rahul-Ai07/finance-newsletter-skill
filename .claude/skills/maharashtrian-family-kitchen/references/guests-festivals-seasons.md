# Guests, Festivals, Fasting & Seasons

## 1. Scaling for guests

Base recipes serve 4 (2 adults + 2 kids ≈ 3 adult portions).

| Total people | Multiply curries, bhaji, usal, varan by | Rice & bhakri/poli | Sweets |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 (family) | ×1 | ×1 | — |
| 6 (+2 guests) | ×1.75 | ×2 | 6 portions + 2 extra |
| 8 (+4 guests) | ×2.5 | ×2.75 | 8 portions + 2 extra |

Rules: spices & salt scale to ~80% of the multiplier (taste and adjust at the end); oil scales to ~70%. Poli: plan 3 per adult guest; bhakri: 2 per adult. Rice: ½ cup raw per adult guest.

### Guest-menu principles
1. Make 1 "showpiece" dish + familiar comfort dishes; don't attempt 6 new dishes.
2. Choose dishes that can be **made ahead** (amti, usal, bharli vangi, shrikhand, kothimbir vadi steamed the day before).
3. Keep it balanced even when festive: one sweet, one fried item max, always a koshimbir and taak/solkadhi.
4. Have a mild option for guest kids.

### Ready guest menus (6–8 people)

**A. Classic Maharashtrian Thali (veg)**
Kothimbir vadi (pan-fried) · Bharli vangi · Batata bhaji · Matki usal · Toor amti · Masale bhaat · Poli / jowar bhakri · Kakdi koshimbir · Shengdana chutney · Shrikhand or sheera · Taak / Solkadhi
*Day before:* hang dahi for shrikhand, steam kothimbir vadi, make bharli vangi masala, sprout matki.
*Day of (2 hrs):* T-120 rice soak & usal; T-90 bharli vangi; T-60 amti, batata bhaji; T-40 masale bhaat; T-20 poli; T-10 fry vadi, koshimbir, taak.

**B. Konkan Lunch (non-veg)**
Fish fry · Malvani fish curry · Solkadhi · Bhaat · Ghavan/ amboli or nachni bhakri · Kala vatana usal (for veg guests) · Kakdi koshimbir · Ukadiche modak (festival) or fruit

**C. Kolhapuri Sunday (non-veg)**
Tambda rassa · Pandhra rassa · Chicken sukka · Bhakri · Jeera rice · Kanda-limbu · Taak — plus veg guests: paneer kolhapuri / mix-veg kolhapuri

**D. Quick surprise guests (45 min, from pantry)**
Kanda poha or upit + chaha (for tea-time) · OR for meal: masale bhaat + kadhi + pithla + poli + kachumber + gul-tup/sheera.

## 2. Upvas (fasting) planning

When an adult fasts, plan **two menus**: upvas plate for the fasting member and normal balanced meals for kids (kids should not skip protein).

Typical upvas-allowed (family rules vary — confirm): sabudana, varai/bhagar, rajgira, shingada flour, potato, sweet potato (ratale), suran, peanuts, dahi, milk, fruits, sendha meeth, jeera, green chilli, tup, kokum, cucumber, dry fruits.

**One upvas day plan:** Morning: milk/fruit + dry fruit · Late-morning: sabudana khichdi + dahi · Afternoon: fruit, rajgira chikki · Evening: bhagar + danyachi amti + kakdi koshimbir · OR upvas thalipeeth + dahi.
Balance tips: pair sabudana with peanuts & dahi (protein), include fruit & cucumber (fibre), drink water/taak, avoid repeated fried upvas foods (sabudana vada, chips).

## 3. Festival calendar & food (dates move with the Marathi calendar — check panchang each year)

| Month (approx.) | Festival | Traditional food | Healthy balance tip |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jan | Makar Sankranti | Tilgul, gulpoli, bajra bhakri, bhogi bhaji (mixed winter veg) | Til & gul are iron & calcium-rich — small pieces daily |
| Feb–Mar | Mahashivratri (upvas), Holi | Upvas food; puran poli, katachi amti | Puran poli with amti + koshimbir, not alone |
| Mar–Apr | Gudi Padwa | Shrikhand-puri, puran poli, kadunimb (neem) chutney | Puri once; rest of day light |
| Apr–May | Akshay Tritiya, summer | Aamras-poli, kairi panha, kokum sarbat | Aamras in place of a sweet, not in addition |
| Jun | Vat Purnima | Upvas food for those observing | — |
| Jul | Ashadhi Ekadashi | Upvas: sabudana, bhagar, rajgira | Protein from peanuts & dahi |
| Jul–Aug | Shravan (veg month for many), Nag Panchami, Narali Purnima | Narali bhaat, patoli (haldi-leaf steamed) | Many veg-only days — plan more pulses, paneer |
| Aug–Sep | Ganeshotsav, Gauri | Ukadiche modak, Gauri jevan (16 bhajya), puran | Steamed modak > fried; portion 2–3 |
| Sep–Oct | Navratri, Dasara | Upvas food, shrikhand-puri on Dasara | Alternate upvas days with full meals for kids |
| Oct–Nov | Diwali | Faral: chakli, chivda, karanji, anarse, besan laddoo, shankarpali | Make baked/air-fried chakli & roasted poha chivda; faral max 1 small plate/day |
| Nov–Dec | Kartiki Ekadashi, Tulsi Vivah, Champa Shashti (Khandoba) | Upvas food; bharit-rodga | Winter greens & bajra season |

## 4. Seasonal produce — Maharashtra (buy local & seasonal: tastier, cheaper, more nutritious)

| Season | Months | Vegetables & greens | Fruits | Cooking focus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Winter (Hivala)** | Nov–Feb | Methi, palak, shepu, mula (radish) & leaves, gajar, green peas (matar), flower, kobi, vangi (for bharit), hurda (Dec–Jan), tur shenga (fresh pigeon peas), harbhara (green chana), valpapdi, lilva | Nagpur santra, peru (guava), awla, bor, chikoo, strawberry (Mahabaleshwar), papaya, sitaphal (till Nov) | Bajra bhakri, tup, til, gul, dink laddoo, vangyache bharit, hurda party, matar usal, gajar halwa, soups |
| **Summer (Unhala)** | Mar–Jun | Dudhi, dodka, tondli, kakdi, bhopla, gavar, raw mango (kairi), kokum | Hapus (Alphonso), Payri mango, jackfruit (fanas), watermelon, karvanda, jambhul, tadgola | Light & cooling: taak, solkadhi, panha, kokum sarbat, kairi chutney, less oil & spice, more koshimbir; make pickles & papad |
| **Monsoon (Pavsala)** | Jun–Oct | Ranbhajya (wild greens: kurdu, takla, sheval, phodshi, kartoli), bhendi, ghosale, padval, makka (corn), aloo | Jambhul, pear, pomegranate, sitaphal (Sep–) | Hot, freshly cooked, well-washed greens; kulith, ginger, pepper; avoid street food & raw salads from outside; corn bhutta, khichdi |

**November note (current season):** Peak time for methi, palak, gajar, matar, Nagpur santra and guava — build plans around these. Start bajra bhakri with til and tup on cold evenings; awla for vitamin C.
