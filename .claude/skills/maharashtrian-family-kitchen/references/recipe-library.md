# Recipe Library — serves 4 (2 adults + 2 kids)

Measures: 1 katori/vati = 150 ml · 1 cup = 240 ml · tsp = teaspoon (5 ml) · tbsp = tablespoon (15 ml).
**Standard phodni (tadka):** 2 tsp oil → ½ tsp mohari (mustard) → crackle → ½ tsp jeera → pinch hing → 8–10 kadipatta → ¼ tsp halad (turmeric). Referred to below as "phodni".
**Goda masala** and **kanda-lasun masala / Kolhapuri masala** recipes are in `weekly-monthly-planner.md` (monthly tasks).

Index: **A. Nashta** (1–14) · **B. Varan/Amti/Usal** (15–24) · **C. Bhaji** (25–36) · **D. Bhakri, Poli, Bhaat** (37–43) · **E. Koshimbir, Chutney, Taak** (44–50) · **F. Non-veg** (51–56) · **G. Upvas** (57–60) · **H. Sweets & guest specials** (61–66)

## A. NASHTA (BREAKFAST)

### 1. Kanda Poha with Peanuts & Peas
⏱ 10 + 15 min · 🌶️ mild · 🥗 iron, carbs, protein from peanuts
**Ingredients:** 2½ cups jada (thick) poha · 2 onions chopped · ½ cup green peas · 3 tbsp peanuts · 2 green chillies · phodni · 1 tsp sugar (optional) · salt · ½ lemon · kothimbir & fresh khobra (coconut)
**Method:** 1) Rinse poha in a sieve 2–3 times, drain; mix in salt, sugar, halad; rest 5 min. 2) Roast peanuts in the phodni oil till crunchy. 3) Add chillies, onion; sauté till translucent; add peas, cover 3 min. 4) Add poha, toss gently on low heat, cover 2 min to steam. 5) Off heat, squeeze lemon; garnish kothimbir & khobra.
**Chef's secret:** Poha should feel soft but separate when pressed — never soggy. Lemon at the end (heat kills vitamin C).
**Kids:** skip chilli; add grated carrot & sweet corn. **Protein boost:** serve with a katori of dahi or add ½ cup sprouted moong.

### 2. Upit (Rava Upma) with Vegetables
⏱ 10 + 15 min · 🌶️ mild
**Ingredients:** 1¼ cups rava (bombay or whole-wheat dalia rava) · 1 onion · 1 cup mixed veg (carrot, beans, peas, capsicum) · 1 tsp urad dal · 1 tsp chana dal · phodni · 3½ cups hot water · salt · lemon · khobra-kothimbir · 1 tsp tup
**Method:** 1) Dry-roast rava till aromatic (not brown); keep aside. 2) Phodni + urad & chana dal till golden; add onion, veg; sauté 4 min. 3) Add hot water & salt; boil. 4) Lower heat, add rava in a stream while stirring. 5) Cover 3 min, add tup, lemon, garnish.
**Chef's secret:** Ratio 1 rava : 2.75–3 water for soft upit; always hot water. **Kids:** add a spoon of tup and cashews.

### 3. Thalipeeth (Bhajani) with Methi
⏱ 10 + 20 min · 🥗 multigrain protein, iron, fibre
**Ingredients:** 2 cups thalipeeth bhajani (or mix: 1 cup jowar + ½ bajra + ¼ besan + ¼ wheat atta + 1 tbsp each roasted urad/moong flour) · 1 onion finely chopped · 1 cup methi/palak finely chopped · 1 tsp each jeera, ajwain, til · 1 tsp chilli powder · ½ tsp halad · salt · water · oil
**Method:** 1) Mix everything; knead soft dough with warm water. 2) On wet plastic/banana leaf, pat a ball into a 6-inch round; make 4–5 holes. 3) Transfer to hot tawa, put ¼ tsp oil in each hole. 4) Cover, cook 2–3 min medium heat; flip; crisp the other side.
**Serve:** with dahi + loni (white butter) or shengdana chutney.
**Kids:** star shapes with a cutter; add grated carrot/beetroot. **Tiffin:** excellent — stays good 5 hours.

### 4. Moong Dal Dhirde (Chilla)
⏱ soak 4 hr / overnight + 20 min · 🥗 high protein
**Ingredients:** 1 cup yellow moong dal (or green moong) soaked · 1-inch ginger · 2 green chillies · 2 tbsp rava/rice flour · salt · ½ tsp jeera · chopped onion, tomato, kothimbir, grated paneer (optional)
**Method:** 1) Grind soaked dal with ginger, chilli to smooth batter (little water). 2) Add rava, salt, jeera; consistency like dosa batter. 3) Spread a ladle on hot greased tawa; sprinkle onion-tomato-kothimbir. 4) Drizzle oil, cook till golden; flip 1 min.
**Kids:** stuff with grated paneer + cheese, roll it — "moong pizza". **Serve:** green chutney / tomato chutney.

### 5. Besan Dhirde (Pith Dhirde)
⏱ 5 + 15 min · 🥗 protein
1½ cups besan + ¼ cup rice flour + 1 onion + 1 tomato + kothimbir + ½ tsp ajwain + halad, chilli, salt + water to pourable batter. Rest 10 min. Cook like dhirde above. Add grated lauki (dudhi) or palak for hidden veggies.

### 6. Nachni (Ragi) Satva / Porridge
⏱ 10 min · 🥗 calcium, iron — great for kids
3 tbsp nachni flour roasted in 1 tsp tup till aromatic → add 1 cup water, whisk lump-free → cook 3–4 min → add 1½ cups milk, 1–2 tsp gul (jaggery) or 2 chopped dates, pinch elaichi → boil 2 min. Top with almond-cashew powder. Serves 2 kids; double for 4.

### 7. Ukadpendi (Vidarbha)
⏱ 20 min · 🌶️ medium · 🥗 fibre
**Ingredients:** 1½ cups wheat atta (or jowar flour) · 1 onion · phodni (with 1 tsp jeera, garlic 5 cloves) · 1 tsp chilli powder · 1½ cups taak/curd-water · salt · kothimbir
**Method:** 1) Roast flour dry till aromatic, light brown. 2) Phodni + garlic + onion. 3) Add roasted flour, mix. 4) Sprinkle taak/water little by little, stirring constantly, till crumbly-soft. 5) Cover 3 min on low steam. Serve hot with lemon and dahi.

### 8. Misal (Weekend special — Puneri or Kolhapuri)
⏱ soak/sprout matki 1–2 days + 45 min · 🌶️ spicy (kids' portion separately) · 🥗 protein, fibre
**Usal:** 2 cups sprouted matki, 1 onion, 1 tomato, phodni, 1 tsp goda masala, ½ tsp chilli, salt — cook 10 min with 1 cup water (pressure cook 1 whistle).
**Kat / Tarri:** 2 onions + ½ cup dry khobra roasted dark + 6 garlic + 1-inch ginger → grind. Heat 3 tbsp oil, add paste, cook till oil separates; add 2 tsp Kolhapuri/kanda-lasun masala, 1 tsp chilli powder; add 4 cups hot water, salt; boil 10 min till red oil floats.
**Assemble:** usal + a little poha/potato → kat on top → farsan, onion, kothimbir, lemon. Serve with pav (whole-wheat pav when possible) or slice bread.
**Healthier:** less farsan, more onion-tomato-kothimbir; one pav each for kids. Kids: usal + dahi without kat.

### 9. Ghavan (Konkani rice crepe) with Coconut Chutney
1½ cups rice flour + ¼ cup wheat atta (optional) + salt + water to thin batter → pour on very hot greased tawa from outside in (lacy holes) → cover 1 min → no flip needed. Serve with coconut chutney or chatani + gul-amba. Protein: serve with matki usal or egg bhurji.

### 10. Amboli (Malvani fermented crepe)
Soak 1 cup rice + ¼ cup urad dal + 2 tbsp chana dal + ½ tsp methi 6 hr; grind; ferment overnight. Cook thick on tawa like uttapa. Probiotic & protein. Serve with kala vatana usal or coconut chutney.

### 11. Sabudana Khichdi (also upvas)
⏱ soak 5–6 hr + 15 min · see Upvas section (#57).

### 12. Phodnichi Poli / Phodnicha Bhaat (leftover rescue)
Leftover poli torn in small pieces (or 3 cups rice) → phodni + onion + green chilli + peanuts → add poli/rice + salt + pinch sugar + halad → sprinkle water/taak → cover 2 min → lemon + kothimbir. Add 2 beaten eggs or 1 cup sprouts for protein.

### 13. Sprouted Moong / Matki Salad (Usal chaat)
2 cups steamed sprouts + onion + tomato + cucumber + grated carrot + pomegranate + chaat masala + lemon + roasted peanuts. 5 minutes, raw-crunchy, high protein. Steam sprouts for young kids.

### 14. Egg options (if eggs allowed)
- **Egg bhurji:** phodni + onion + tomato + chilli + halad + 6 eggs scrambled, kothimbir. Serve in poli roll for tiffin.
- **Masala omelette with veggies:** 2 eggs per adult, 1 per child + onion, tomato, palak, capsicum.
- **Anda poli roll:** omelette cooked on top of poli on the tawa, rolled with ketchup-free green chutney.

## B. VARAN, AMTI, USAL (PROTEIN)

### 15. Sadha Varan (everyday plain toor dal)
⏱ 5 + 20 min (pressure cooker 3–4 whistles) · 🥗 protein
1 cup toor dal washed + 3 cups water + ¼ tsp halad + ½ tsp oil + pinch hing → pressure cook → whisk smooth → add salt, ½ tsp jaggery (optional), water to pouring consistency → boil 2 min.
**Classic serving:** varan-bhaat + tup + limbu (the Maharashtrian comfort plate). Kids love it — no chilli.

### 16. Toor Dal Amti (Goda masala, Puneri style)
Cooked toor dal (from 1 cup) + phodni (with kadipatta, 1 green chilli) + 1 tsp goda masala + ½ tsp chilli + 2 kokum or 1 tsp chinch (tamarind) pulp + 1 tbsp gul + salt + water → boil 8 min → fresh khobra & kothimbir. Sweet-sour-spicy balance is the soul of amti.

### 17. Katachi Amti (with puran poli)
Use the "kat" (water) left after boiling chana dal for puran + some cooked dal; phodni + 1 tsp goda masala + kokum + gul + salt; boil. Thin, aromatic. Zero waste.

### 18. Dal Palak / Methi Varan
Cooked toor or moong dal + phodni with 5 garlic + 2 cups chopped palak/methi (cook 3 min) + tomato + salt + lemon. One pot: protein + greens + iron + vitamin C.

### 19. Matki Usal
⏱ sprouts + 25 min
2 cups sprouted matki + phodni + 1 onion + 1 tomato + 1 tsp goda masala + ½ tsp chilli + 2 tbsp grated khobra (or khobra-onion paste) + 1½ cups water + salt + gul pinch → cook covered 12–15 min (or 1 whistle) → kothimbir, lemon. Pair with bhakri or poli.

### 20. Chawli (Black-eyed beans) Usal
1 cup chawli soaked 6 hr → pressure cook 3 whistles. Phodni + onion + garlic + tomato + 1 tsp kanda-lasun masala + cooked chawli + water → simmer 10 min.

### 21. Kala Vatana Usal (Malvani)
1 cup black peas soaked overnight, cooked 5–6 whistles. Masala: ½ cup dry khobra + 1 onion roasted + 5 garlic + 1 tsp Malvani/goda masala → paste. Cook with peas 10 min. Weekend with amboli or ghavan.

### 22. Pithla (Besan curry — the 15-minute saviour)
⏱ 15 min · 🥗 protein
½ cup besan whisked in 2 cups water (lump-free) · phodni with 6 garlic crushed + 2 green chillies + 1 onion · add besan water, salt, halad, stir continuously on medium heat 6–8 min till thick but pourable · kothimbir.
**Variations:** methi pithla, palak pithla, kanda-tomato pithla, **zunka** (drier, 1 cup besan sprinkled on sautéed onion). **Pitla-bhakri + thecha + kanda** = classic Maharashtrian dinner.

### 23. Kulith (Horse gram) Pithla / Usal
High protein & iron, warming in winter/monsoon. Roasted kulith flour pithla like #22, or soaked kulith usal like #19.

### 24. Rajma / Chhole (weekly protein variety)
Soak overnight, pressure cook till fully soft (rajma: 6–8 whistles — **must be fully cooked**). Onion-tomato-ginger-garlic masala + cooked beans + water → simmer 15 min. With bhaat or poli.

## C. BHAJI (VEGETABLES)

### 25. Methichi Patal Bhaji (Fenugreek in besan-buttermilk)
2 bunches methi chopped + phodni + garlic + chilli → sauté → add 2 tbsp besan in 1 cup taak + 1 cup water + 2 tbsp peanuts (soaked) + salt + gul → simmer 7 min. Iron + protein. Serve with bhaat or bhakri.

### 26. Palakchi Patal Bhaji
Same as #25 with palak + chana dal (soaked) + peanuts. Add a spoon of lemon at end.

### 27. Methi / Shepu / Lal Math Sukhi Bhaji
Phodni + 6 garlic + 1 onion + chopped greens (2 bunches) + ¼ cup soaked moong dal + salt → cover 5 min → khobra. Quick, iron-rich.

### 28. Bharli Vangi (Stuffed Brinjal — guest favourite)
⏱ 20 + 30 min
**Ingredients:** 8–10 small purple brinjals slit in plus-shape · Stuffing: ½ cup roasted peanuts, ¼ cup dry khobra roasted, 2 tbsp til roasted, 1 onion roasted, 1 tbsp goda masala, 1 tsp chilli, 1 tbsp gul, 1 tsp chinch pulp, salt, kothimbir → coarse grind.
**Method:** 1) Stuff brinjals. 2) Phodni (3 tsp oil), add brinjals, roll to coat. 3) Add leftover stuffing + 1½ cups water. 4) Cover & cook on low 15–20 min till brinjal is tender, gently turning once. 5) Gravy should be thick, oil separating.
**Chef's secret:** roast peanuts, khobra and til separately — each to its own perfect colour.

### 29. Batata Bhaji (Dry potato — kids' favourite)
4 boiled potatoes cubed + phodni + 2 green chillies + halad + salt + kothimbir + lemon. Add green peas or carrot for colour. Use in poli rolls.

### 30. Bhendi (Okra) Bhaji
Dry okra fully before chopping. Phodni + onion + okra (no lid, medium heat, 10 min) + 1 tsp goda masala + 1 tbsp peanut powder + salt at the end. Not slimy.

### 31. Kobi (Cabbage) Bhaji with Chana Dal
Phodni + 2 tbsp soaked chana dal + finely chopped cabbage + green peas + salt + 2 tbsp fresh khobra. 12 min.

### 32. Flower (Cauliflower)–Batata–Matar Rassa
Onion-tomato masala + cauliflower + potato + peas + 1 tsp goda masala + water → simmer 15 min. Good with poli.

### 33. Dudhi (Lauki) Chana Dal Bhaji
Dudhi cubes + soaked chana dal + phodni + goda masala + tomato + salt → cook till soft. Light dinner bhaji.

### 34. Gavar (Cluster beans) / Farasbi (French beans) Bhaji
Chopped beans + phodni + garlic + goda masala + peanut powder + salt; cover-cook 10 min.

### 35. Dodka (Ridge gourd) Bhaji with Peanuts
Peeled dodka cubes + phodni + green chilli + peanut powder 2 tbsp + salt + khobra. Light, quick.

### 36. Mix Vegetable Kolhapuri (weekend)
Carrot, beans, peas, cauliflower, capsicum + kolhapuri paste (roasted onion, khobra, til, garlic, kolhapuri masala) — medium spicy. Take kids' veg out before adding masala; mix with butter + a little cream-free cashew paste.

## D. BHAKRI, POLI, BHAAT (GRAINS)

### 37. Jowar Bhakri
2 cups jowar flour + salt (optional) + warm water → knead each ball fresh with heel of palm (dough must be smooth) → pat on floured board to 7-inch → place on hot tawa, wipe top with wet hand → flip after 1 min → finish directly on flame till it puffs. Serve with tup or loni.
**Tips:** knead small batches; warm water is key for bajra & nachni.

### 38. Bajra Bhakri (winter)
Same as jowar; warm water; with til sprinkled on top. Best with thecha, pithla, vangyache bharit, loni and gul.

### 39. Nachni (Ragi) Bhakri / Poli
Nachni + little wheat atta (3:1) for easier rolling. Calcium powerhouse.

### 40. Poli (Phulka / Ghadichi poli)
2½ cups atta + pinch salt + 1 tsp oil + water → soft dough, rest 20 min. Ghadichi poli: roll, smear oil, fold into triangle/fold twice, roll again → soft layered poli. Allow 2–3 per adult per meal, 1–2 per child. Add methi/palak puree to dough for **methi/palak poli**.

### 41. Masale Bhaat (Spiced rice — Sunday / guests)
⏱ 15 + 25 min
1½ cups rice soaked 20 min · 1 cup tondli (ivy gourd) or vangi/flower/matar · phodni with 2 tej patta, 4 cloves, cinnamon · 1 onion · 2 tsp goda masala · 1 tsp chilli · 3 cups hot water · salt · 1 tbsp tup · khobra & kothimbir & cashews.
Fry veg in phodni, add rice and masala, toast 2 min, add hot water, salt; cook covered on low 15 min (or 2 whistles). Rest 5 min. Serve with tup, kadhi or solkadhi, and koshimbir.

### 42. Moong Dal Khichdi + Kadhi (light dinner)
¾ cup rice + ½ cup moong dal + phodni + vegetables (carrot, peas, dudhi) + halad + 4½ cups water + salt → 3 whistles. Top with tup.
**Maharashtrian kadhi:** 1 cup dahi + 2 cups water + 2 tbsp besan whisked + ginger-chilli + salt + 1 tsp sugar; phodni with jeera, kadipatta, methi seeds; simmer 8 min, don't boil hard.

### 43. Varan-Bhaat plate
Rice (hand-pounded or ambemohar, ½ katori per adult with plenty of varan) + varan (#15) + tup + limbu + koshimbir + bhaji. Daily anchor.

## E. KOSHIMBIR, CHUTNEY, TAAK

### 44. Kakdi (Cucumber) Koshimbir
2 cucumbers chopped + 2 tbsp roasted peanut powder + green chilli + salt + pinch sugar + lemon + 2 tbsp dahi (optional) + phodni of jeera. Add just before serving.

### 45. Gajar (Carrot) Koshimbir
Grated carrot + peanut powder + lemon + salt + jeera-mohari phodni. Kids love it sweet with a pinch of sugar/raisins.

### 46. Shengdana (Peanut) Lasun Chutney (dry)
1 cup roasted peanuts + 6 garlic + 1 tsp chilli + salt → coarse grind. Lasts 2 weeks. Protein + healthy fat.

### 47. Javas (Flaxseed) Chutney
1 cup javas roasted + 2 tbsp til + 6 garlic + chilli + salt + pinch chinch → grind. 1 tsp daily for adults = omega-3.

### 48. Thecha (Green chilli-garlic, for adults)
10 green chillies + 10 garlic + kothimbir roasted in 1 tsp oil + salt + peanuts → crush. Very spicy — adults only, small quantity.

### 49. Taak (Spiced buttermilk) & Solkadhi
**Taak:** 1 cup dahi + 3 cups water + salt + roasted jeera powder + ginger + kothimbir; phodni of jeera-kadipatta optional.
**Solkadhi:** 10 kokum soaked in ½ cup water 30 min → squeeze; 1 cup thick coconut milk + kokum water + 1 garlic crushed + green chilli + salt + kothimbir. Cooling, digestive; pairs with fish and masale bhaat.

### 50. Tomato–Kanda Kachumber / Beet Koshimbir
Any raw seasonal veg + lemon + salt + peanut powder. Add one raw item to every lunch.

## F. NON-VEG (only on allowed days — check family-profile.md)

### 51. Malvani Fish Curry (Bangda / Surmai / Pomfret)
⏱ 20 + 25 min
500 g fish (cleaned) marinated with halad, salt, 1 tsp lemon 15 min · Paste: 1 cup fresh khobra + 4 kashmiri chillies + 1 tbsp coriander seeds + 6 peppercorns + 6 garlic + ½ onion + 1 tsp chinch pulp. Cook paste in 2 tbsp oil 5 min, add 2 cups water + 4 kokum + salt; boil; slide fish in, simmer 6–8 min (don't stir — swirl the pan). Serve with bhaat + solkadhi.
**Omega-3:** bangda (mackerel) and tarli (sardine) are affordable and best. Kids: debone carefully, serve with plain rice and less gravy.

### 52. Fish Fry (Tawa, Rava coated)
Marinate fish slices in ginger-garlic paste, halad, chilli, salt, lemon → coat in rava + rice flour → shallow fry 3 min each side in 2 tbsp oil total. (Shallow-fried counts as half a fried item.)

### 53. Kolhapuri Tambda Rassa (Chicken/Mutton)
750 g chicken · marinade: dahi, ginger-garlic, halad, salt · Kolhapuri paste: 2 onions + ½ cup dry khobra roasted dark + 1 tbsp til + 8 garlic + 2 tsp Kolhapuri masala → grind. Cook paste in 3 tbsp oil till dark & oil separates; add chicken, sear 5 min; add 3 cups hot water, simmer 20 min (mutton: pressure cook 5–6 whistles). Kids' portion: take chicken out before adding extra masala, serve with **Pandhra rassa**.

### 54. Pandhra Rassa (White, mild — perfect for kids)
Chicken stock (from 300 g chicken/bones with ginger-garlic, 1 tej patta, whole spices) + paste of 1 cup fresh khobra + 10 cashews + 1 tbsp til + ginger-garlic + 2 green chillies + kothimbir → simmer 10 min. Soothing, rich, mild.

### 55. Egg Curry (Andyacha Rassa)
6 boiled eggs, scored + onion-tomato-khobra masala + 1 tsp goda/kanda-lasun masala + water → simmer 10 min. With poli or bhaat.

### 56. Chicken Sukka (dry, kid-friendly mild version)
Boneless chicken + ginger-garlic + halad + mild masala + roasted khobra + tup — cooked dry. Good for tiffin in poli roll.

## G. UPVAS (FASTING) — no onion, garlic, grains, rice, pulses; check family's own rules

### 57. Sabudana Khichdi
⏱ soak 5–6 hr + 15 min
2 cups sabudana (rinse 2×, soak with water just covering) · ¾ cup roasted peanut powder · 2 potatoes boiled cubed · 2 green chillies · 1 tsp jeera · 2 tbsp tup · salt (sendha meeth) · 1 tsp sugar · lemon · kothimbir
Mix sabudana with peanut powder, salt, sugar. Tup + jeera + chilli + potato → add sabudana → stir gently on medium 5–7 min till translucent → lemon, kothimbir. Serve with dahi (protein!).
**Tip:** press a pearl — should squash easily. Wet sabudana = sticky khichdi.

### 58. Bhagar (Varai) + Danyachi Amti
**Bhagar:** 1 cup varai washed + tup + jeera + chilli + 2½ cups hot water + salt → cook covered 15 min. **Danyachi amti:** ½ cup roasted peanuts ground + 2 cups water + kokum + jeera-tup phodni + chilli + salt + gul → boil 5 min. Classic Ekadashi lunch.

### 59. Rajgira / Upvas Thalipeeth
Rajgira (amaranth) or upvas bhajani flour + boiled mashed potato + peanut powder + jeera + chilli + salt → thalipeeth on tawa (#3). Rajgira = calcium + protein.

### 60. Ratalyacha Kees / Upvas Koshimbir / Fruit
Sweet potato grated, cooked in tup + jeera + chilli + peanut powder. Plus kakdi-peanut koshimbir with dahi, fruit, and nuts. Upvas plate must still have protein (peanut, dahi, rajgira, milk).

## H. SWEETS & GUEST SPECIALS (occasional — sugar budget!)

### 61. Puran Poli (festivals: Holi, Gudi Padwa, Pola)
Puran: 1 cup chana dal cooked soft (save water for katachi amti) + 1 cup gul → cook till thick → elaichi + jaiphal → mash/grind. Dough: 1½ cups atta (or half maida) + oil + water, very soft, rest 1 hr. Stuff, roll thin with rice flour, roast on tawa with tup. Makes ~10.

### 62. Shrikhand (Amrakhand in mango season)
1 kg dahi hung 6 hr → 300 g chakka + ½ cup powdered sugar (or less) + elaichi + kesar milk → whisk smooth. With puri (guests) or poli.

### 63. Rava–Besan Laddoo / Dink Laddoo (winter)
Dink laddoo: dink (edible gum) fried in tup, crushed + roasted wheat atta/khobra + dry fruits + gul — 1 small laddoo/day in winter for kids. High calorie: limit to one.

### 64. Ukadiche Modak (Ganeshotsav)
Rice flour dough + filling of fresh khobra + gul + elaichi → shape → steam 12 min. Serve with tup.

### 65. Kothimbir Vadi / Alu Vadi (guest starter)
Kothimbir vadi: 2 cups kothimbir + 1 cup besan + 2 tbsp rice flour + ginger-chilli + til + salt → steam as a log 15 min → cool, slice → **pan-fry or air-fry** instead of deep-fry.

### 66. Sheera / Gajar Halwa (light dessert)
Sheera with rava + tup + milk + gul + banana/pineapple. Gajar halwa in winter (Nov–Feb) with milk & minimal sugar.
