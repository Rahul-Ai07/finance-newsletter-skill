# Template: Re-engagement / Win-back

Use when: dormant users, churn-risk customers, lapsed subscribers.

## Structure
1. **Subject line** — acknowledge the gap honestly or lead with what's new/changed since they left; avoid guilt-tripping ("We miss you!" underperforms specificity)
2. **Preheader** — the single reason to come back right now
3. **Opening** — brief, no-blame acknowledgment of inactivity + what's different now (new feature, improved rate, resolved pain point)
4. **Value reminder** — 1-2 concrete reasons the product/service is worth returning to, ideally personalized (their past usage, a feature they hadn't tried)
5. **Primary CTA** — low-friction re-entry: "Log back in," "See what's new," rather than a hard conversion ask
6. **Incentive (optional)** — a genuine, time-bound reason to return now, if one exists — subject to the same RBI guardrails as `promotional.md` if it involves rates or credit
7. **Opt-down option** — offer reduced-frequency emails or clear unsubscribe rather than only all-or-nothing — reduces spam complaints and preserves list health
8. **Footer** — standard footer + compliance disclaimer if incentive involves rates/credit

## Notes
- Segment by reason for lapsing where known (price-sensitive vs. feature-gap vs. simply forgot) — the same generic "come back" message underperforms across all three.
- This format tolerates more direct, personal tone than promotional or market-update formats — it's a relationship repair, not a pitch.
