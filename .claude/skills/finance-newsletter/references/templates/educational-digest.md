# Template: Educational Digest

Use when: thought leadership, market insights, financial literacy content, advisor-audience newsletters.

## Structure
1. **Subject line** — the single most interesting insight, framed as news or a question, not "This week's newsletter"
2. **Preheader** — second-most-interesting point, to add reason to open
3. **Hook** — 1-2 sentences establishing why this topic matters right now
4. **3-5 insights** — each as a short section:
   - Heading (the takeaway, not the topic — "Rate cuts likely delayed to Q4" not "About interest rates")
   - 2-4 sentences of substance per insight
   - Cite source/data where used
5. **RKnomics Spotlight** (recurring segment) — one of the 3-5 insight slots, or a dedicated section immediately after them, framed as research/commentary attributed to RKnomics. Treat it with the same rigor as any other insight: a specific takeaway heading, substance, and cited data — not a generic plug.
6. **Resource links** — deeper reading, tools, calculators (optional, 1-3 max)
7. **Soft CTA** — this format is relationship-building, not conversion-focused; CTA should be low-commitment ("Reply with your questions," "Share with a colleague," "Book a portfolio review")
8. **Footer** — standard footer + disclaimers on any market commentary or forward-looking statements

## Notes
- This is the one template where a "hard sell" CTA feels out of place and can damage trust with an audience reading for insight, not a pitch. Keep the ask light.
- Advisor/institutional audiences tolerate (and often prefer) more density and jargon here than in consumer-facing formats — don't over-simplify for this audience.
