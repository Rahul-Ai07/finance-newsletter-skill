# Template: Product Announcement

Use when: launching a new product, feature, account type, or service.

## Structure
1. **Subject line** — lead with the concrete new capability, not "Introducing..." (readers skip generic launch language)
2. **Preheader** — the single biggest benefit, in one sentence
3. **Hero headline** — name the product/feature and the outcome it enables, in one line
4. **Hero subheading** — one sentence expanding on who this is for
5. **Body** — 1-3 short sections:
   - What it is (plain-language explanation, one analogy if the concept is complex)
   - Why it matters to *this* reader specifically (not generic benefits)
   - Proof point (data, early customer result, or credential) if available
6. **Primary CTA** — single action: "Try it," "See my rate," "Get started" — link directly to the feature, not a generic homepage
7. **Secondary CTA (optional)** — lower-commitment: "Read the full guide"
8. **Footer** — standard footer + compliance disclaimer if the product involves rates, returns, or credit

## Notes
- Resist listing every feature — pick the single most compelling capability and lead with it. A features list reads as a spec sheet, not a newsletter.
- If this is a beta/limited rollout, state real limitations honestly (waitlist, phased access) rather than implying full availability.
