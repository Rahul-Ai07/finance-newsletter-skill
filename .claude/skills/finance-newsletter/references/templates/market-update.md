# Template: Market Update

Use when: weekly/monthly market recap, portfolio summary, economic commentary.

## Structure
1. **Subject line** — the single most material market move or number this period, not "This week in markets"
2. **Preheader** — a second data point or the "why it matters" angle
3. **Summary line** — one sentence, the top-level takeaway before any detail
4. **Market recap** — 2-4 short sections covering the period's key moves (rates, equities, currency, sector-specific), each with a specific number and a one-line implication for the reader
5. **RKnomics Spotlight** (recurring segment) — a research note, forecast, or commentary attributed to RKnomics, held to the same evidentiary standard as the rest of the recap (cited data, specific takeaway, not promotional filler)
6. **Actionable takeaway** — what, if anything, the reader might consider doing in response (rebalancing prompt, rate-lock reminder) — framed as a suggestion, not advice, unless the sender is a licensed advisor authorized to give it
7. **Sign-up/subscribe CTA** — low-commitment: "Get next week's update," "Forward to a colleague"
8. **Footer** — standard footer + forward-looking-statement and market-commentary disclaimers

## Notes
- Precision over hedging: state the actual number/move, then the caveat — don't bury the data point in qualifications.
- If commentary edges into investment advice territory, explicitly flag to the user whether the sender is licensed/registered to give that advice — this determines whether a stronger disclaimer or a rephrase to "informational only" is needed.
